🛍 Shopping plan

Your shopping priorities Watches · affordable luxury · anime · manga · gaming · electronics · figures · toys · winter jacket · shoes · Onitsuka Tiger.
⌚ Watches

For watches, Tokyo is the strongest part of your trip. Check both new and pre-owned stores and compare condition, box/papers, warranty and tax-free eligibility.

Ginza Luxury watch boutiques and department stores. Best for Rolex, Omega, Grand Seiko and other luxury brands. Open Google Maps →
Akihabara Good area for pre-owned watches and electronics-related shopping. Open Google Maps →
Ueno / Ameyoko Excellent area to compare second-hand watches and bargain-oriented stores. Open Google Maps →
💎 Affordable luxury

For luxury at lower prices, concentrate on Japanese pre-owned stores rather than only flagship boutiques.

Ginza Best for luxury boutiques, department stores and premium Japanese brands. Open Google Maps →
Shinjuku Large department stores and pre-owned luxury retailers. Open Google Maps →
Shibuya Good combination of fashion, Japanese brands and contemporary luxury. Open Google Maps →
🎌 Anime / Manga / Figures

Your two major anime shopping zones are Den Den Town in Osaka and Akihabara in Tokyo.

Den Den Town Anime, manga, figures, Pokémon, Nintendo, retro games and arcades. Open Google Maps →
Akihabara Tokyo's strongest area for anime, manga, figures, games and electronics. Open Google Maps →
Mandarake Excellent for pre-owned manga, collectibles, figures and older merchandise. Open Google Maps →

💡 Important trip notes

🚆

Osaka → Kyoto

1 Nov: check out of Smile Hotel Shinosaka, then transfer to Wander Kyoto Nanajo Hotel.

🚄

Kyoto → Tokyo

5 Nov: take the Shinkansen. Reserve seats in advance, especially if you want Fuji-side views.

🌅

Shibuya Sky

6 Nov: sunset is the ideal time. Book a timed ticket in advance.

🌿

Arashiyama

3 Nov: keep your 06:30–07:00 target. The bamboo grove becomes much busier later.

🎟️

Ghibli Museum

8 Nov is a fixed booking. Advance admission is required. Build the rest of the day around the museum.

🛍️

Shopping timing

Your strongest dedicated shopping day is 7 Nov in Akihabara, with additional luxury/fashion shopping on 6 and 9 Nov.
