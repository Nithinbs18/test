Important verified notes

  • Manga Museum: open 10:00–17:00, portrait registration starts at 10:00 and fills first-come; the museum is closed on Wednesdays, including 4 Nov.
  • Oi Racecourse flea market: currently scheduled for Sat 7 and Sun 8 Nov, 09:00–14:30; rain cancels. This plan uses Sun 8 Nov.
  • Yachiyo: beside Nanzenji, garden-focused restaurant; reserve the plant-based Shojin Yudofu set and request garden-facing seating.
  • BRAND OFF Shijo Kawaramachi: useful because it stays open later than temples and is close to your Gion/Kawaramachi route.
  • Pokémon Café: reservation-only and currently full for your target dates; keep as cancellation-only.
  • Vegetarian caution: in Japan, apparently vegetable-only dishes may still use fish dashi. Ask specifically about fish/bonito stock when the restaurant is not vegan/vegetarian.
